# Artificial Neural Networks Coursework

This repository contains my assignments, implementations, experiments, and study notes developed as part of the **Artificial Neural Networks** course in the **Master's Degree in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at the Universidad de Guadalajara**.

## About this repository

This repository documents my learning process throughout the course and includes practical implementations of the concepts covered in class.

The main purpose is to strengthen my understanding of artificial neural networks by combining theoretical study with hands-on programming, experimentation, and analysis.

The contents may include:

* Neural network implementations.
* Programming assignments.
* Mathematical exercises.
* Model training and evaluation.
* Experiments with different architectures and parameters.
* Data preprocessing and visualization.
* Personal notes and observations related to the course topics.

## Learning Objectives

Through this repository, I aim to:

* Strengthen my understanding of the mathematical foundations of neural networks.
* Implement neural network algorithms from scratch when appropriate.
* Understand the learning and optimization processes behind neural models.
* Analyze model behavior through practical experiments.
* Compare different architectures, activation functions, and training strategies.
* Build a structured record of my progress throughout the course.

## Repository Structure

The repository will be organized according to the topics, assignments, and exercises covered during the course.

Each folder or notebook may contain:

* The problem or topic being studied.
* The corresponding implementation.
* Experiments and results.
* Notes and conclusions derived from the exercise.

The structure may evolve as new topics are introduced throughout the semester.

## Technologies

Depending on the exercise, this repository may use tools such as:

* Python
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Pandas
* Jupyter Notebook
* TensorFlow / Keras
* PyTorch

Some exercises may also include implementations developed from scratch to better understand the internal mechanisms of neural networks.
